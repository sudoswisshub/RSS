Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Our RSS Reader is designed with a privacy-first approach. We believe your reading habits should remain private. This policy outlines the minimal data we interact with.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information. All your subscription data, read/unread states, and settings are stored locally on your device or synced via your private iCloud account.

  • Subscriptions: Stored locally on your device.
  • Articles: Cached temporarily for offline access.
  • Analytics: We do not use third-party tracking or analytics.

Third-Party Services

The app fetches content from RSS feeds provided by third parties. When you open an article, you are interacting directly with the content provider's website. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the websites you subscribe to.

iCloud Sync

If enabled, your data is synced using Apple's iCloud service. This data is encrypted and only accessible by you. We never have access to your iCloud data.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through our Support Page.
